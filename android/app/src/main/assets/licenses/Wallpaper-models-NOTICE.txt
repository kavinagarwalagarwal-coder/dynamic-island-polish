Dynamic Droid offline wallpaper recognition

EfficientNet-Lite0 (ImageNet 1,000-class classifier)
Provider: Google AI Edge / MediaPipe models
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_classifier
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_classifier/efficientnet_lite0/float32/1/efficientnet_lite0.tflite

DeepLab-v3 (PASCAL VOC semantic segmentation)
Provider: Google AI Edge / MediaPipe models
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/deeplab_v3/float32/1/deeplab_v3.tflite
SHA256: ff36e24d40547fe9e645e2f4e8745d1876d6e38b332d39a82f0bf0f5d1d561b3

These pretrained models have finite supported categories and can misclassify images.
All inference runs on the device; no wallpaper images are uploaded.
The model files retain their embedded original metadata, notices and labels.
